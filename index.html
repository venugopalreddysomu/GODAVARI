   How a threshold gets chosen. The ADXL367 wakes this node when the deck
   moves more than a set amount away from how it normally sits —
   gravity and bracket tilt are removed in hardware, so the number below is a
   deviation, not an absolute acceleration. Profile the quiet bridge,
   profile a train, and the separation between the two peaks is what the
   threshold sits in.

        The ADXL367 waits for an inactivity event after each train. If the deck
        never settles below the quiet threshold — wind, road traffic, a
        following train — it stays asserted and no further train

        registers have drifted. It never runs during a capture or an upload.

        Stress per kN is the setting that turns fatigue on. It converts
        measured load into the stress range at the monitored detail, and it can
        only come from the structural engineer for that specific member and
        connection — the firmware has no way to derive it. While it is 0
        the node still accumulates damage so the pipeline is exercised, but it
        reports UNCALIBRATED and the figure has no physical meaning.
      

    

  


  

    
Cumulative fatigue — rainflow cycles by load range

    

      
    

    

      
      
      

      
    

    

    

      Clearing is permanent and cannot be undone from here. Accumulated
      damage is the running total of every train this node has ever weighed, and
      it is the one figure on the device that nothing else can reconstruct —
      everything else is either a live reading or recoverable from the cloud.
      Clear it when the node is commissioned onto the structure, so bench trials
      do not count, or when it moves to a different member. The S-N curve, the
      calibration, the queued events and the lifetime counters are all kept.

  1. Old node still answers? Connect to it, Read from node, then Export file. Keep the file with the site records.
    2. 
        
  2. Connect to the new node and provision it with the same device label (Cloud tab).
    3. 
        
  3. Import file, check the New value column, then Apply to node.
    4.

    5.

        number. A replacement that starts again at 1 would have its events thrown away
        as repeats of the old node’s until it passed them.

Tilt X / Y—

      
Change since install—

      

        
        
        
      

      

        Tilt is only meaningful while the node is still. The angles come
        from the direction of gravity, so during a train — or with a hand on
        the enclosure — they measure the deck's motion instead and are
        refused rather than shown.
        


        Judge by the change, not the absolute. The part's zero-g offset
        puts about a degree of error on the absolute angles, but it is the same
        error every time, so it cancels almost entirely in the difference:
        a 0.3° change since commissioning is real, while a 0.3° absolute
        reading is not. A drift here with no change in load is a bracket
        loosening or a bearing rotating.
        


        Every angle here is an average of 32 samples, not a single
        reading. The part carries about 2 mg of broadband noise, which near
        level is 0.13° — enough that a motionless node appears to wander
        by a quarter of a degree. Averaging 32 divides that by √32, giving
        about 0.02°. The Noise row is the spread seen while
        averaging: around 2 mg is the sensor itself, and much more than that
        means something is physically shaking.
      

      

        A part lying still should read about 1000 mg magnitude —
        that is gravity, and it is the quickest proof the part is alive, level
        and scaled correctly.
